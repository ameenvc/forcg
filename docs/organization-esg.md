# Organization ESG readiness — model 1.0

The authenticated `/user/score` replaces the legacy volume-based score and hard-coded global benchmark. It reads this organization's installation database, observes module-enable flags, and exposes aggregates only. It sends nothing to customers and makes no automatic qualification decision. No migration is required.

`config/esg_score.php` is the reviewed, versioned rule registry. Pillar weights E/S/G are 35/35/30. Indicator weights sum to 100 per pillar. Each connected indicator measures satisfactory workflow statuses divided by all non-cancelled, non-archived, non-deleted records. Blank status earns zero; missing schema and empty tables have no observed score. Missing or disabled indicators retain their headline denominator and earn no points; they are never treated as proof of good performance. Headline readiness = sum(pillar weight × indicator weight × observed score / 10000). No evidence at all yields no score, not zero performance or perfect performance. Pillar observed results renormalize only connected weights and must always be read with coverage.

This measures internal workflow readiness, not emissions, social impact, control effectiveness, assurance, certification, an ISO conformity statement or customer approval. Scope is all current stored eligible records; there is no claim of annual comparability, prior-month trend or historical snapshot retention. Deleting/archiving records can change denominators; a tamper-resistant evidence ledger is not provided. Incident counts themselves never earn or lose points. Workflow completion proxies are not outcome measures.

GHG inventory, resource consumption, labour rights and anti-bribery/supply-chain evidence remain explicit unconnected requirements. All 24 module names are shown in coverage; modules lacking validated mapping are supplementary rather than silently rewarded. Customer/industry materiality, verified reporting periods, evidence expiry, independent assurance and approval histories require customer policy and additional structured measurement sources. They cannot be inferred from this score. Do not use observed-only pillar scores as customer pass/fail rules.

Any stored legal `Not Complied` record is an explicit qualification blocker. Otherwise the result remains provisional and requires customer review, regardless of score. This is a conservative internal review flag, not a representation of a customer's legal acceptance policy. There are no customer-specific thresholds or automatic approvals. CSV and print/PDF expose model version, coverage, snapshot time and provisional status. Exports remain authenticated and private/no-store; no public vendor passport is published.

Deploy controller, service, registry, Blade and CSS together. Versioned asset URL avoids filesystem timestamp failures. Existing auth/MFA on UserController applies. Clear Blade cache if the deployment serves the former view. Schema mismatches are shown as unavailable and logged without source contents. Tests cover weighted denominators, blank/missing/disabled sources, deletion/cancellation, blockers, all-module coverage and view claims.
